====Various Setting Explanations====
===Classes===
==Parameter Curves==
Max HP: Health (always 500, in engine overridden to be Toughness * 100)
Max MP: Endurance (always 100)
Attack: Power (always 5)
Defense: Armor (always 0)
M.Attack: -NOTHING- (always 0)
M.Defense: -NOTHING- (always 0)
Agility: Agility (always 5)
Luck: -NOTHING- (always 0)


==Traits==
=Sp-Parameter=
Physical Damage: Toughness, damage is divided by this number (Always 5%, actually represents a whole number)
Floor Damage: Balance, attacks that only inflict stress are reduced by this (Always 5%, actually represents a whole number)

=Ex-Parameter=
TP Regeneration: Focus, determines stress recovered at turn start (Always 5%, actually represents a whole number)
Hit Rate: Melee Accuracy (Always 5%, actually represents a whole number)
Evasion Rate: Evasion (Always 5%, actually represents a whole number)
Critical Rate: Ranged Accuracy (Always 5%, actually represents a whole number)
Magic Evasion: Special Accuracy, ranged accuracy but without weapon bonuses (Always 5%, actually represents a whole number)
Magic Reflection: Parry, like a critical fail chance for a melee attacker, which inflicts stress on the attacker (Always 1%)


===Skills===
==General Settings==
MP Cost: Does nothing.
TP Cost: Inflicts stress on the user.
Hit Type: Physical Attack is Melee, Magical Attack is Ranged (for Attack and Techniques) or Special (for all other skills)


==Damage==
Type: HP Damage targets health, MP damage inflicts stress.
Element: Does what you expect, but Trip doesn't really do anything here.
Formula: Does nothing for Attack and weapon Techniques, otherwise is a flat value that represents attack power.


==Effects==
Gain TP: Reduces the target's stress.


===Weapons===
==General Settings==
Weapon Type: Everything before Throw is a melee type. Throw and onward is strictly ranged. Reach and ranged weapons can attack without penalty when attacking the enemy back row from your front row, or the enemy front row from your back row. Attacking the enemy back row from your back row incurs half the penalty it normally would. 


==Traits==
Attack Element: Piercing turns part of the damage that is resisted by armor into more damage. HighCrit simply has an easier time bypassing armor altogether. A weapon with neither, to be effective, should simply have a relatively high attack power value. The remaining Attack Elements are elemental damage types. Each elemental damage type reprents 10% of the weapon's total attack power.

Add Skill: Use this to add relevant skills to the weapon, such as techniques (4 to 102 in the Skills section of the database) that represent other ways to attack with the weapon. For instance, the MeleThrw weapon types should be given the Throw skill.


===Armors===
==Traits==
=Ex-Parameter=
Critial Evasion: Coverage. The higher this is, the lower chance that an attack will bypass armor. At 85% or above, only high crit attacks can hope to get through, as other attacks raise the targets effective coverage by 15% for the attack.
